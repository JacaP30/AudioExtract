AudioExtract bundles third-party software. Use AudioExtract only with recordings
and materials you have the right to process. The app runs locally, creates no
user accounts, and does not collect personal data. Donations (Buycoffee) are
optional.

This folder contains license texts for bundled components:

- FLASK-BSD.txt — Flask (BSD-3-Clause)
- YT-DLP-UNLICENSE.txt — yt-dlp (Unlicense)
- FFMPEG-LGPL.txt — FFmpeg, typically LGPL when shipped via imageio-ffmpeg

AudioExtract itself is licensed under the MIT License (see ../LICENSE).
